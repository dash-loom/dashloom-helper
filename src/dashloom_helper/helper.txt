# Dashloom Helper — Analytics Reference

Project: dashloom-helper
Purpose: Standardize product analytics calculations and report checks.
Traffic metrics: sessions, unique users, page views, conversions.
Revenue metrics: gross revenue, refunds, net revenue, monthly recurring revenue.
Subscription metrics: active subscriptions, new subscriptions, cancellations.
Conversion rate (%) = conversions / sessions * 100 (when sessions > 0).
Average revenue per user = net revenue / active users (when active users > 0).
Net revenue = gross revenue - refunds.
Period comparison (%) = (current - previous) / abs(previous) * 100 (when previous != 0).
Data checks: reject invalid dates, duplicate event IDs, and negative counts.
Reporting: show date range, timezone, currency, data source, and last refresh.
Alerts: flag missing data, sharp metric changes, and failed data validation.
Note: Reference guidance only; adapt field names and integrations to your Dashloom implementation.
